FAQ

What's the cheapest way to the Acropolis?
The X95 bus at €5.50 — about €3.50 cheaper than the Metro, and it runs 24 hours a day.
What's the fastest way?
The Metro, roughly 52 minutes total including the walk, and traffic doesn't affect it.
Is there transport at night?
Yes — the X95 runs 24/7, less often after 22:00. The Metro stops around 23:34.
How much does it cost and how do I pay?
Metro €9, X95 €5.50, contactless card on both.
