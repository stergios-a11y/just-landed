The cheapest, simplest ways from Heraklion Airport (HER) into the city and to the ferry port — by public KTEL bus, with live departures and no taxi upsell.

Getting into town

The city bus is the cheapest way to the centre and the port — €1.30 from the kiosk (€2.30 from the driver), every ~10–15 min, about a 20-minute ride. It's frequent but doesn't run after midnight, and it's cash only. We show the available schedule — confirm at the stop.

FAQ

How do I get from Heraklion Airport to the centre?
Take the city bus, about 20 minutes to the centre and port. €1.30 from the kiosk, €2.30 from the driver.
What's the cheapest way?
The city bus at €1.30 (ticket from the kiosk) — the most economical option.
Is there a night service?
The bus runs roughly 06:00–00:00. Nothing after midnight, so arrange a taxi.
