Live times, not estimates

The X93 times here come live from OASA telematics whenever buses are running — the “in X min” is the real next arrival. The X93 links the airport directly with the Kifisós and Liossíon intercity KTEL coach terminals.

FAQ

How do I get from the airport to the Kifisós coach station?
Take the X93 bus direct for €5.50, no change. It also stops at Liossíon. It runs 24 hours a day.
Is it good for intercity KTEL coaches?
Yes — the X93 drops you right at the intercity coach terminals for the rest of Greece, without going into the centre.
Is there a night service?
Yes — the X93 runs 24/7, less frequently overnight.
How much does it cost?
€5.50 with a contactless card onboard.
