Athens Airport → Piraeus Port

Public transport from Athens Airport (ATH) to Piraeus Port — compare X96, Metro and taxi options before your ferry.

Where are you headed?

Faster or cheaper?

Catching a ferry with luggage? The X96 drops you right at the port gates, no change, for €5.50. The Metro is a touch faster but needs a line change at Monastiráki (Line 3 → Line 1).

Live times, not estimates

The X96 times here come live from OASA telematics whenever buses are running — handy when you're racing for a ferry. The Metro runs on a fixed schedule, so we show its exact times. Change the departure time above to see how the fastest option shifts.

FAQ

How do I get from the airport to Piraeus port for my ferry?
The X96 bus goes direct to the port gates, no change, for €5.50. It runs 24 hours a day.
What's the fastest way?
The Metro is slightly faster (~61 min) but needs a change at Monastiráki (Line 3 → Line 1).
Is there transport at night?
Yes — the X96 runs 24/7, ideal for early-morning ferries.
Will I make my ferry?
Budget ~90 minutes on the X96 and check the live arrival above. To be safe, leave with comfortable time before departure.
