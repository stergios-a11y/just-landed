How to get from Chania Airport (CHQ) into town by public KTEL bus — fare, timetable and live departures, no taxi upsell.

Getting into town

The KTEL bus runs direct into Chania town for €2.50 in about 30 minutes. Departures are sparse (~8–10 a day) and not timed to flights — the next time we show is an estimate, so check the posted schedule at the stop. Cash to the driver.
