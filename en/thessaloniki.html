How to get from Thessaloniki Airport (SKG) into the city centre by public OASTH bus — fare, frequency and departures, no taxi upsell.

Getting into town

The OASTH 01X bus is the cheapest and simplest way into the centre — €2, every ~25 min, 24/7, dropping you at Aristotelous and the White Tower. It also stops at the railway station and KTEL Makedonia. It isn't on a live feed, so we show scheduled times — confirm at the stop.

FAQ

How do I get from Thessaloniki Airport to the centre?
Take the OASTH 01X bus, €2, direct to Aristotelous/White Tower in about 40 minutes. It runs 24/7 (01N overnight).
How much does it cost?
€2 — the special airport fare. Buy at the arrivals machines or onboard (no change given).
Is there a night service?
Yes — the 01X/01N runs 24/7, roughly every 30 minutes overnight.
