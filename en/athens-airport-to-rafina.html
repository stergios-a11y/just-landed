FAQ

How do I get from the airport to Rafina port?
Take the KTEL bus direct for €3 in about 40 minutes, from Exits 2–3 on the Arrivals level. It's the cheapest airport bus.
How much does it cost?
Just €3 — the cheapest connection from the airport. Pay with a contactless card onboard.
Where do Rafina ferries go?
Rafina serves Andros, Tinos, Mykonos and Evia — often faster than Piraeus for the northern Cyclades.
Is there a late service?
KTEL runs roughly 05:00–22:00. Outside those hours, a taxi is your option.
